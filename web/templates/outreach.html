Outreach

What is Outreach?

Outreach generates LinkedIn search URLs and DM templates for a job. It does NOT send messages automatically — it helps you find the right recruiter and craft a message manually.

How it works:

  1. Click Outreach on any job card in the Jobs page
  2. System generates a LinkedIn search URL for the company + relevant role titles
  3. System drafts a personalized DM template based on your profile config
  4. You open LinkedIn, find the right person, and send the message yourself

Note: LinkedIn requires login to view people. The search URL opens LinkedIn where you can manually find contacts. DMs are drafts — edit before sending.
